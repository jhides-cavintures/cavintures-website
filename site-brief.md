# CAVINTURES Website Redesign Brief

**Status:** Approved by owner. This brief records the owner's answers; it is not a design or build plan.

## Business

CAVINTURES sells both general merchandise and customized merchandise. The current website describes customized apparel, promotional products, awards, and IT products, as well as general merchandise. The owner confirmed both parts of the business are accurate.

General merchandise is the primary focus for the redesigned site. The categories the owner wants featured are:

- Household essentials
- Electronics
- Furniture
- Office supplies
- costumized clothes

The site should also continue to represent the customized merchandise side of the business.

## Customers and service area

- The site should serve all kinds of buyers; the owner has not narrowed this to a specific buyer segment.
- CAVINTURES sells or delivers within the Philippines.
- Customers should be able to place orders through online checkout.

## Purpose of the redesign

The main goal is to bring in more sales or inquiries. The website should help a broad range of buyers discover products and complete orders online.

The owner says CAVINTURES stands out through all of the following: broad product range, competitive pricing, quality, and convenience.

## Pages and information

The owner confirmed customers should be able to find:

- Product shopping and online ordering, including checkout
- Delivery and returns information
- Contact details
- Company information

The exact page names and full page list are still to be confirmed. Core shopping pages such as product categories, product details, cart, and checkout are implied by the requested online ordering flow, but have not been individually confirmed.

## Style and visual direction

- Clean and modern
- A balanced color palette: not too colorful, but not too muted
- white and dark navy blue and also muted orange
- Use a different palette from the existing navy-and-orange look shown in the attached CAVINTURES banner.
- No reference websites were provided.

## Current website and supplied images

The current website presents both customized merchandise and broad general merchandise, which matches the owner's clarification that both are correct. Its general-merchandise description currently names household essentials, electronics, fashion, beauty, and furniture; the owner specified a different feature set for the redesign: household essentials, electronics, furniture, and office supplies.

The supplied images show workwear/safety items, bags, and a CAVINTURES-branded executive bag. They are visual material only; they do not establish additional product categories or instructions for the redesign.
